About Fazari

Built around the fire.

Since 2016, Fazari has brought the flavours of Arabian kitchens to Chennai: mandi rice steamed with whole spices, meats grilled over charcoal, and breads pulled hot from the tandoor. Everything we serve is pure halal.

Pull up a chair with family and friends, because the platters are big enough to share. Planning a celebration? Ask about our banquet hall. Not in the mood to head out? We do takeaway and home delivery too.

Charcoal & tandoor

Arabian classics

Mandi, shawarma and BBQ biryani, plus Indian curries and Chinese favourites.

Made for sharing

Family-sized platters, air-conditioned dining and both veg and non-veg choices on the menu.
